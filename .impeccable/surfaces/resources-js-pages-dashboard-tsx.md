# Dashboard (cliente y consultor)

Mode: Operate. Users: CMK consultant, client SST lead and client manager, equally (PRODUCT.md).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The dashboard answers two questions in order: how healthy is this company, and what do I do today. It refuses the category default of a grid of equal stat cards where every module shouts at the same volume.

OWN-WORLD: CMK's incumbent world. Navy (`--primary`, hsl 220 48% 17%) carries the hero band; warm off-white page; Poppins for headings, Inter tabular for figures. Status uses dot + word (never color alone) (rojo = acción, ámbar = pronto, verde = en meta, gris = sin dato). No side stripes, no eyebrow kickers, no nested cards.

STORY: The manager reads the hero sentence and the 0312 ring and leaves knowing the state. The SST lead and the consultant go down one prioritized list and open the record. The areas strip mirrors the sidebar, so the map in the head is the same as the menu.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width navy band: company name, one summary sentence of counts, the 0312 ring with its Res. 0312 band at the right. Below, two columns: "Qué hacer hoy" (wide, merged agenda + alerts, top 6) and "Salud del sistema" (narrow: compliance rows, 0312 indicators with real 12-month series, unmeasured collapsed to one line). Consultant view: navy portfolio band, then clients ranked by urgency beside the consolidated to-do list.

FORM: Health + Today. Authority: the user's explicit choice on 2026-11-11 among three wireframes presented through the structured question tool (Health + Today, By sidebar areas, Inbox of actions). The skill's seeded concept roll and decision page were skipped and the user was told; there is no seed key. Do not re-roll: the user's pick stands.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
